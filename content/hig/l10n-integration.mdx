# Localization & accessibility integration

These two integration concerns share one rule: **they are build-time
requirements, not post-release tasks.** The detailed workflows live in the
[Localization & Accessibility](/localization/) section; this page is the HIG
contract.

## Localization contract

<Checklist>
  - All user-visible strings go through gettext (or the toolkit equivalent)
    from the first commit, no string concatenation that breaks word order.
  - Persian (`fa`) translations ship for first-party apps at release time;
    English is the source, Persian is not an afterthought.
  - RTL layout follows the locale automatically (see [RTL & Persian](/hig/rtl-and-persian/)).
  - Translations are tested in the app, not in translation tools alone ,
    reflow, truncation, and mixed-direction rendering only show at runtime.
</Checklist>

## Accessibility contract

<Checklist>
  - Accessibility tree (ATK/AT-SPI or QAccessible) exposes every control's
    name, role, and value from day one, it cannot be bolted on.
  - Full keyboard operation is a release blocker (see [Accessibility](/hig/accessibility/)).
  - Both themes pass contrast checks on real content.
  - The app respects system font scaling and reduced motion.
</Checklist>

## CI hook

The portal's own checks treat these as build gates (see
[Testing & QA](/testing/)): a translation-coverage check for required
languages, and an accessibility smoke test over primary flows.
