# Dialogs & toasts

## Dialogs

Dialogs interrupt for a decision. They are modal, centered, and escape with
<kbd>Esc</kbd> or the close button. A dialog has at most three actions, and
the confirming action carries the intent's styling.

## Destructive flows

Destructive actions follow a strict sequence: the trigger button is
destructive-styled only **inside** the confirmation dialog; the entry point
looks like a normal button until the user commits.

## Toasts

Toasts report the outcome of a background operation. They auto-dismiss after
~4 seconds, stack bottom-end, and never block input.

## Rules

<Checklist>
  - Dialogs state the consequence before the action (“This will remove the
    Parch Welcome application and its 3 dependencies”).
  - Cancel is always available in destructive dialogs.
  - Toasts are not for decisions, anything requiring a choice is a dialog or
    a banner.
  - Don't stack more than three toasts; coalesce repeats (“3 mirrors failed”).
</Checklist>
