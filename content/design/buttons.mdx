# Buttons & chips

Buttons trigger actions. Chips classify and filter, they are not buttons.

## Rules

<Checklist>
  - Exactly **one** accent button per view.
  - Destructive buttons are only destructive on confirmation, the confirming
    dialog repeats the destructive styling (see [Dialogs](/design/dialogs/)).
  - Loading replaces the label with an ongoing action; the button disables
    itself to prevent double submits.
  - Minimum hit target: 32px height (small) with ≥ 8px spacing; 36px for
    primary flows.
</Checklist>

## Chips

Chips are compact status and category labels. They never look like buttons and
are never the only place a critical state is communicated.
