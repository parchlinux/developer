# Materials: radii, borders & elevation

Parch surfaces are quiet. Separation comes from 1px hairlines and surface
color shifts first; shadows are reserved for things that truly float.

## Rules

<Checklist>
  - Nested radii follow the geometry: inner radius = outer radius − padding.
  - Borders and shadows never both on the same element at rest.
  - Dialogs and popovers are the only elements that may cast `--shadow-raised`
    at rest.
  - Hover raises by one step: surface → surface-2, or border-strong; no scale
    jumps larger than 2%.
</Checklist>

## Elevation in dark mode

Dark mode reads elevation through **lightness**, not shadow: background →
surface → surface-2 get progressively lighter. Shadows still exist but are
barely visible; do not compensate by darkening borders everywhere.
