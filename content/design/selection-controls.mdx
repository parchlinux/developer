# Selection controls

Choosing the right control is a semantics decision:

<Checklist>
  - **Switch**, an effect applies immediately (a setting flips live).
  - **Checkbox**, one or more independent options; effect applies on commit.
  - **Radio group**, exactly one choice from 2–5 visible options.
  - **Dropdown**, one choice from many, or when space is tight.
</Checklist>

## Rules

<Checklist>
  - A switch that requires “Save” to apply is a checkbox, don't fake instant
    behavior.
  - Show a parent checkbox as **indeterminate** when children are partially
    selected.
  - Radio and checkbox hit targets include the label, not just the control.
  - Dropdowns keep the selected item marked with a check and close on Esc.
</Checklist>
