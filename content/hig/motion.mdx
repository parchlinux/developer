# Motion

Motion in Parch is feedback: it confirms an action happened or shows where a
change came from. It never entertains.

<Checklist>
  - **Durations:** 150ms for hover/press/state changes; 250ms for enter/exit
    transitions (dialogs, drawers, toasts).
  - **Curve:** soft-out `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`. Nothing bounces.
  - **Transform and opacity only.** Never animate width, height, or layout
    properties, they jank and fight resize.
  - **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`:** the portal disables all transitions
    under it; applications must do the same (GTK: `gtk-enable-animations`;
    Qt: platform theme reduced-motion flag).
</Checklist>

## What may animate

- Pressed/hover states on buttons and rows.
- Drawer and dialog enter/exit.
- Progress bars and indeterminate sweeps.
- Cross-fades between peer views (optional, 150ms).

## What may not

- Loops other than the indeterminate progress sweep.
- Decorative parallax, pulsing brand elements, animated gradients.
- Entrance animations on data the user asked to see (loading spinners already
  communicate the wait).
- Anything over 300ms.
